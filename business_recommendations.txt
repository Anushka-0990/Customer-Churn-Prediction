BUSINESS RECOMMENDATIONS — Customer Churn Reduction

=======================================================

1. CONTRACT TYPE is a top churn driver.
   Month-to-month customers churn at 42.7%, vs 2.8% for the most stable contract type.
   -> Recommendation: Offer a discounted incentive for month-to-month customers to migrate to 1-year contracts (e.g., 5-10% loyalty discount).

2. TENURE strongly predicts churn risk.
   Customers in their first tenure bracket churn at 47.4%, the highest of any group.
   -> Recommendation: Build a structured onboarding/retention journey for the first 12 months (proactive check-ins, early-tenure discounts, satisfaction surveys).

3. SERVICE ENGAGEMENT (add-ons) reduces churn.
   Customers with 0 add-on services churn at 21.4%, while those with the most add-ons churn at 5.3%.
   -> Recommendation: Bundle Online Security / Tech Support into a free trial for at-risk, low-engagement customers to increase stickiness.

4. Top 5 predictive features from the model: tenure, TotalCharges, Contract_Two year, InternetService_Fiber optic, MonthlyCharges
   -> Recommendation: Prioritize a targeted retention campaign for customers who score high-risk on these features — model output can feed directly into a CRM 'at-risk' flag.

5. ESTIMATED BUSINESS IMPACT
   Out of 7,043 customers, 1,869 churned (26.5%). Even a modest 10% reduction in churn among high-risk, flagged customers would retain roughly 186 additional customers.
